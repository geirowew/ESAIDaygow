The future

As AI takes on more of the routine work of writing code, the craft of programming will move up a level. Developers will spend more time shaping ideas, setting clear constraints, and deciding whether a solution is useful, safe, and worth maintaining. The future of coding is not less human; it is more focused on the questions only people can ask well.
